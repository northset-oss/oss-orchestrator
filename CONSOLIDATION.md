The shared spawn-based `run`/`git`/`ghJson` command helpers, hashing and canonical manifest logic, binding and OSS identity/DCO checks, receipt footer and PR-body builder, candidate/URL/spec validation, timeline recheck, and bounded pool moved into `core.mjs`; `oss.mjs` now contains only the canonical CLI and prepare pipeline (including changed-file classification and receipt construction), while `ship.mjs` retains the proven resumable attestation/ledger/fork/PR pipeline and imports shared logic from `core.mjs`. The relevant identity, footer, timeline, validation, prepare, receipt, binding, and fixed manifest-serialization coverage now lives in `oss.test.mjs`; the superseded two-pass runner, its old-only tests, and implementation notes were removed, with no behavior change to prepare or ship.
